Privacy Policy

Codewright Guild · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains what information Codewright Guild collects when you sign in and buy from this website, and what we do with it. We keep it to the minimum needed to take payment and deliver what you bought.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, provided by Google when you choose “Sign in with Google”. We never see your Google password.
  • Order details: the plan you chose, the amount, the date and time, and PayPal’s order or invoice reference.

We do not see or store your card number, PayPal password, or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by PayPal.

How we use it

  • To confirm who is buying, using the email Google verified.
  • To create your PayPal checkout or invoice for the correct price.
  • To email you your payment confirmation and purchase details.
  • To keep a record of orders and deal with support requests or payment problems.

Who handles your data

  • Google – provides sign-in.
  • PayPal – processes payments and invoices under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend – delivers our confirmation emails to you.
  • Cloudflare – securely routes traffic between this site and our payment server.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts this website.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for support, accounting and legal reasons. After that they are deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons). You can also remove this site’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
